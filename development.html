04Public realm

Public Space Framework

A welcoming civic ground
for everyday life.

Shape the spaces around the municipal hall as a connected, comfortable extension of the community: easy to reach, pleasant to spend time in, and flexible enough for daily visits and shared events.

01

Clear arrival

Make entrances, public routes, and service destinations easy to see and understand.

02

Shade and comfort

Use trees, covered edges, and places to sit to support a more comfortable visit.

03

Green connections

Bring planting and stormwater-friendly landscapes into the public realm.

04

Inclusive access

Plan welcoming, step-free paths and gathering areas for different ages and abilities.

05

Active edges

Let public-facing services and useful amenities add life to the spaces around the hall.

06

Room to gather

Keep civic open spaces adaptable for everyday use, community programs, and events.

06Integrated public services

New One Stop Shop

City services,
all in one place.

Give residents a clear, welcoming starting point for municipal transactions, information, and support—so they can find the right service without navigating the whole building.

01

Arrive and get oriented

A visible welcome point helps visitors understand where to go and what to bring.

02

Reach the right service

Organize public-facing counters and assistance around an easy-to-follow service journey.

03

Wait with comfort

Provide accessible seating, clear queue information, and helpful guidance while residents wait.

07Daily life

Daily Life at Civic Center

A civic place that works
throughout the day.

The public spaces can support more than appointments. A mix of comfortable places to pause, meet, and take part in community life can make the municipal hall feel welcoming beyond office hours.

Morning

Start with ease

Clear paths, shaded seating, and an easy-to-find welcome point help people begin their visit.

Midday

Pause and connect

Green, comfortable outdoor spaces offer room to wait, rest, or meet a neighbor.

Evening

Make space for community

Flexible gathering areas can host civic programs and community activities when scheduled.

09Design approach

Three ideas shape the proposal

01

Open ground

A civic plaza extends the public realm and gives the community a flexible place to meet, wait, celebrate, and connect.

02

Climate-wise comfort

Deep overhangs, planted edges, and cross-ventilated public spaces make a resilient building suited to its tropical context.

03

Clear civic journey

One visible, welcoming address brings people intuitively from arrival to the service, assembly, and administration spaces beyond.
