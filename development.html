Three ideas shape the proposal

01

Open ground

A civic plaza extends the public realm and gives the community a flexible place to meet, wait, celebrate, and connect.

02

Climate-wise comfort

Deep overhangs, planted edges, and cross-ventilated public spaces make a resilient building suited to its tropical context.

03

Clear civic journey

One visible, welcoming address brings people intuitively from arrival to the service, assembly, and administration spaces beyond.
